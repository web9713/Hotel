Our Story

26Years of Grace

Since 1998

Hospitality Crafted With Intention

BCA Hotel began as a single boutique property with a simple conviction: that true luxury lies in attentiveness, not excess. Over 26 years, that conviction has grown into a destination trusted by travellers from over 60 countries.

Today, our 64 rooms and suites, signature restaurant and spa continue to be shaped by the same philosophy — thoughtful design, genuine service and an unwavering respect for our guests' time.

Our Mission

To deliver effortless, five-star experiences that feel personal, not performed.
