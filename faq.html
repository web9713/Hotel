Complimentary breakfast is included with all direct bookings made through our website or booking page.

Bookings can be cancelled or modified free of charge up to 48 hours before check-in. Please contact our reservations team for assistance.

Yes, we offer complimentary valet parking for all registered guests, available 24 hours a day.

We welcome small, well-behaved pets in select rooms with prior notice. Additional cleaning fees may apply.

Yes, BCA Hotel is fully wheelchair accessible, including accessible rooms, ramps and elevators throughout the property.

High-speed WiFi is complimentary and available throughout all rooms and public areas.

Yes, departure transfers can be booked at the front desk or through your room concierge tablet at least 3 hours in advance.
