Meet The Chef

Rajiv Kapoor, Executive Chef

Trained in Paris and shaped by two decades across award-winning kitchens, Chef Rajiv brings a modern coastal sensibility to BCA Hotel's table — where local produce meets classical technique.

His philosophy is simple: let the ingredient lead. Each seasonal menu is built around what arrives fresh that morning, from the coast and from our own kitchen garden.
